### English
**Gofatho AI (Goofatho Doona)** is the first offline-capable AI translator, trilingual dictionary, everyday phrasebook, and synchronized dual-script Audio Bible built for the **Gofatho language (`ISO 639-3: gof`)** spoken across the Gofa Zone (Sawula, Bulqi, Laha, Beto, Melo Koza, Demba Gofa, Zala, Geze Gofa, and Oyda) in South Ethiopia.

- **6-Way Bi-Directional Translator**: Translate between **Gofatho (Latin)**, **Gofatho (Ethiopic Fidel `ኦራꬃ ጫቁዋ`)**, **Amharic (`አማርኛ`)**, and **English** with live morphological breakdowns.
- **100% Offline SQLite FTS5 Lexicon & Everyday Phrasebook**: Instant search across **1,334 verified dictionary headwords** and **120 everyday conversational phrases** across 8 domains (Greetings, Market, Health/Clinic, Farming, School, Transport, Family, and Law).
- **Synchronized Dual-Script Audio Bible**: Read and listen to all **27 New Testament books (260 chapters, 7,931 verses, and 22,017 sub-verse phrase audio clips)** with side-by-side Gofatho Latin, Fidel, Amharic, and English.
- **Gofatho Voice AI (ASR & TTS)**: Powered by fine-tuned **Meta MMS-1B (`gof-script_latin`)** speech recognition preserving phonemic glottal stops (`7` = `/ʔ/`) and **Meta MMS-TTS VITS** neural speech synthesis.
- **Community Field-Testing ("Suggest Better Translation")**: Submit and upvote dialect-aware corrections directly from your phone.
