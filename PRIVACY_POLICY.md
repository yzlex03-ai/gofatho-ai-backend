# Privacy Policy — Gofatho AI (`et.gofazone.gofatho_ai`)

**Effective Date**: November 3, 2026

## 1. Offline-First Data Architecture
Gofatho AI is engineered as an **offline-first mobile application**. The complete Gofatho dictionary (`1,334 entries`), everyday phrasebook (`120 phrases`), and quad-parallel New Testament (`7,931 verses`) are stored locally on your Android device inside an SQLite database (`gofatho_ai_master.sqlite`). Dictionary lookups and offline translations never leave your device.

## 2. Microphone & Voice Recognition (`RECORD_AUDIO`)
When you press and hold the **Push-to-Talk** microphone button in the Voice AI tab, audio is captured solely to transcribe spoken Gofatho into text (`gof-script_latin` ASR). Voice recordings are never sold or shared with third-party advertisers.

## 3. Community Translation Feedback ("Suggest Better Translation")
When you voluntarily submit a translation improvement via the **Suggest Better Translation** feature, only the source phrase, your suggested Gofatho translation, and your selected woreda dialect (e.g., Sawula, Bulqi, Laha) are transmitted to the Gofatho AI corpus server to improve future language models for the Gofa community.
